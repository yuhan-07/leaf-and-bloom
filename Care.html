PLANT CARE GUIDE

Keep Your Plants Happy & Healthy.

Good plant care comes down to three things: the right light, the right amount of water, and the right soil. Start here, and your plants will thank you.

Learn the Basics

THE BASICS

Light, Water, and Soil

Get these three right and most plants will thrive.

STEP 1

Light

Light is a plant's food. Most indoor plants prefer bright, indirect light, while some flowering and outdoor plants need full sun.

  • Place plants near a bright window
  • Turn pots now and then for even growth
  • Move plants back if leaves look scorched

STEP 2

Water

More plants are lost to too much water than to too little. Check the soil first and water only when it needs it.

  • Push a finger 2 to 3 cm into the soil
  • Water slowly until it drains out the bottom
  • Empty any water left in the saucer

STEP 3

Soil & Pot

Roots need air as well as water. Loose, well-draining soil and a pot with drainage holes keep them healthy.

  • Always choose a pot with drainage holes
  • Use a mix made for your type of plant
  • Repot when roots crowd the container

BEGINNER FAVORITES

Easy-Care Plants to Start With

Forgiving plants that are happy with simple routines.

LOW MAINTENANCE

Snake Plant

Tolerates low light and irregular watering, which makes it a great first plant.

Light: Indirect light
Water: When soil dries

FAST GROWER

Golden Pothos

Trailing vines grow quickly and tell you clearly when they are thirsty.

Light: Bright, indirect
Water: When topsoil dries

SUCCULENT

Aloe Vera

Stores water in its thick leaves, so it prefers to be watered less often.

Light: Bright light
Water: When soil dries

TROUBLESHOOTING

What Is My Plant Telling Me?

Common signs and simple ways to fix them.
Common plant problems, their likely causes, and fixes
What you see Likely cause What to try
Yellow leaves Too much water or poor drainage Let the soil dry out and check the pot's drainage holes.
Brown, crispy tips Dry soil, dry air, or too much direct sun Water more regularly and move the plant away from harsh sun.
Drooping leaves Thirst, or roots sitting in water Feel the soil: water if it is dry, or let it dry out if soggy.
Long, stretched stems Not enough light Move the plant closer to a bright window.
No flowers Too little light or no resting period Give more bright light and avoid overwatering.

QUICK ANSWERS

Plant Care Questions

Tap a question to read the answer.
How often should I water my plant?

There is no fixed schedule, because it depends on light, season, and pot size. Check the soil with your finger and water when the top layer feels dry.

Do my plants need fertilizer?

A light feeding during the growing season can help, especially for flowering plants. Follow the label directions and never feed a plant that is stressed or very dry.

When should I repot?

Repot when roots grow out of the drainage holes or circle tightly around the root ball. Choose a pot only slightly larger than the old one.

Are these plants safe for pets?

Several popular houseplants, including peace lily, monstera, and pothos, can irritate pets if chewed. If you have cats or dogs, ask us before you choose.
